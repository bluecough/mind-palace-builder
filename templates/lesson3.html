Lesson 3: Building Vivid Associations

"Make it vivid" is easy to say and hard to do on the first try. Concretely, a strong association usually has two ingredients: a sense (something it sounds, smells, or feels like) and motion or action (something happening, not just sitting there). A giant golden key stuck in your door is okay. A giant golden key screeching as it turns and sparks fly across the doorframe is much stickier.
