Lesson 4: Encoding Hard Content

Shopping lists are easy -- they're already concrete objects. Numbers, names, and abstract ideas aren't, so you need a bridge that turns them into something picturable before you place them.

Numbers: the consonant code

Each digit maps to a consonant sound; vowels are silent and free to use however you like. A number becomes a word, and a word is easy to picture.

0123456789
s, zt, dnmr ljk, c, q, gf, vp, b

Example: 32 → "m" + "n" → a word like MooN. Picture a moon, and you've encoded 32.

Abstract ideas: person-action-object

For a concept with no shape -- justice, freedom, entropy -- invent a concrete scene: a specific person doing a specific action to a specific object. The scene stands in for the idea.
