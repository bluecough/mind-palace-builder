Lesson 2: Choosing & Sizing Your Route

Pick a physical space you know so well you could walk through it with your eyes closed -- your home, your commute, your childhood house. A few rules that separate routes that work from ones that don't:

  • 5-8 stops per palace is a good starting size -- enough to hold a real list, not so many you lose track of the order.
  • Distinct, well-spaced spots. "Kitchen counter" and "kitchen sink" three feet apart will blur together. Spread stops out physically.
  • One direction, always. Walk the same route the same way every time -- front door, then hallway, then kitchen, never backwards.
  • Use a different palace for each unrelated list. Reusing one palace for a grocery list and a speech outline causes the two sets of images to collide in your memory.

Practice: draft a route

List at least 5-8 stops, one per line, in the order you'd actually walk them.
