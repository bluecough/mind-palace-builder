Lesson 5: Avoiding Mistakes & Spaced Review

Common mistakes

  • Images that aren't distinctive. Two similar associations blur together during recall -- the fix is to make each one weirder and more specific, not just "different."
  • Thin associations. "A key at the door" is a fact, not an image. It won't survive a day.
  • Overloading a locus. One vivid item per spot. If you need more capacity, add more stops instead of stacking items.
  • Never reviewing. Even a great palace fades without review -- that's not a flaw in the technique, it's just how memory works.

Spaced review

Every time you quiz yourself, each location moves through review "boxes": get it right and the next review is pushed further out (1, 2, 4, 7, 14, then 30 days); get it wrong and it resets to daily review. The homepage shows how many locations are due today -- that's the queue to work through, not the whole palace every time.
